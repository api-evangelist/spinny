## Spinny Overview
Spinny is India’s most trusted platform for buying and selling used cars, offering a fully transparent, customer-first experience. Every Spinny Assured car undergoes a 200-point quality check, comes with a 1-year warranty, 5-day money-back guarantee, and hassle-free ownership transfer.

## Key Features
- **Buy Used Cars**: Explore Spinny Assured cars across hatchbacks, sedans, SUVs, and luxury cars.
- **Sell Used Cars**: Get instant online car valuation, doorstep car evaluation, and same-day payment.
- **Spinny Max**: Premium platform for buying and selling luxury cars.
- **Car Finance**: Easy EMI options and low-interest car loan facilities.
- **Car Insurance**: Buy or renew car insurance with competitive rates.
- **RC Transfer**: 100% safe and seamless ownership transfer.
- **Scrap Car Service**: Sell your old car for scrap with fair valuation.
- **Car Research**: In-depth research pages with specifications, reviews, safety ratings, and comparisons (e.g., Jaguar XF).
- **e-Challan Services**: Check and pay e-Challans across states.
- **Spinny Blog**: Read expert insights, guides, car reviews, and ownership tips.
- **Car repair and servicing**: You can Get your car servicing and repairing service in your nearest city.
- **Car Hub Locations**: Explore nearest Spinny Car Hub Locations for test drive.
- **Partners**: Online auction platform for business partners to buy inspected used cars for vehicle dealership.
- **Service Cost Calculator**: Check accurate car service cost estimates by brand, model & city. 
- **Car Loan EMI Calculator**: Use Spinny's car loan EMI calculator to quickly calculate your monthly car loan EMI, interest rate, and loan repayment terms. 
- **Car Valuation**: Check your car valuation online for FREE with Spinny’s used car value calculator.
- **RTO Details**: Check RTO vehicle registration details online.
